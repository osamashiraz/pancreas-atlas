## P1 — imprecise, overstated or unsourced

- SMAD4 is not "only at invasion" — lost in ~31–41% of PanIN-3 (Wilentz 2000); p16 30/55/71% by grade (Koorstra 2008).
- KRAS allele shares (G12D ~40%, G12C ~1–2%) unsourced; G12R (~15%, PDAC-typical) and KRAS-wild-type (~8–10%) missing.
- KIC/KPIC "no Trp53 change": Ink4a flox also removes Arf → weakened p53 signalling; cite Aguirre 2003 / Bardeesy 2006, not a secondary.
- "Vascular invasion in practically all" — attribute the book quote; perineural near-universal, LVI variable.
- PanNEC "not a high-grade version" hides WHO 2019 PanNET G3; MiNEN "≥30%".
- PDA survival / location / "% metastatic at diagnosis" / metastatic site order differ across surfaces; date-stamp registry numbers.
- `CANCER_COMPARE` "75–80% of resected neoplasms" (Hopkins series) vs ~85–90% of pancreatic cancers.
- Desmoplastic-stroma atlas entry lacks the Özdemir/Rhim caveat; CAF ≠ stellate only (myCAF/iCAF); failed stromal trials.
- Acute pancreatitis severity / infected-necrosis / mortality numbers dated vs revised Atlanta; "diabetes in 70%", pseudocyst 10% vs 20–40% inconsistent.
- Hover claims to verify: islet blood-flow share & fenestrations (rodent), juice volume (1–2 L/day), glucokinase half-max, "many acinar cells binucleate".
- SPINK1 "loss of function" → "variants (commonly N34S) that raise risk".
- "Mucin in a small duct means PanIN" contradicts the ADM mimic.
